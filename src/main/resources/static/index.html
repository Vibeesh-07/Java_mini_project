How it works at PSG iTech

A simple 3-step recovery workflow powered by deterministic matching

1. Report Belonging

Submit details about the lost or found item including color, brand, approximate location, and incident date.

2. Smart Match Score

The backend matching engine evaluates category, keyword similarity, campus zones, and dates with explainable scores up to 100.

3. Secure Claim & Recovery

File a private claim with identifying proof. The finder verifies and approves the claim, returning the item safely.
